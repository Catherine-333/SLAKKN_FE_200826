About iLovePDF

iLovePDF is a software platform from Spain dedicated to managing and editing PDF documents. Operated by ILOVEPDF, S.L. and based in Barcelona, the service provides a broad collection of document utilities through web, desktop, and mobile applications. Its tools cover common PDF tasks such as merging, splitting, compressing, converting, editing, organizing, securing, signing, comparing, and performing OCR on files.

The service was founded in 2010 and developed from a simple website for merging and splitting PDFs into a platform with more than 25 document-management tools. iLovePDF Mobile launched in 2017, followed by iLovePDF Desktop in 2018. This expansion gives users multiple ways to work with PDF files, whether through a browser, a computer application, or a mobile device.
